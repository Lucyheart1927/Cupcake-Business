About Usagi Puffs

Soft, dreamy pastries inspired by cute aesthetics and crafted with small-batch care. Every puff is a little moment of joy.

Our Story

Usagi Puffs began as a cozy home project, baking small batches of cream puffs and cupcakes for friends and family. The pastel colors, delicate glazes, and cute details quickly became our signature. As word spread, we hopped from a tiny home kitchen into a dedicated bake space, keeping our focus on quality ingredients and whimsical finishes.

Today, we’re proud to share fresh pastries daily—always hand-piped, always made with love, and always a little bit magical.
